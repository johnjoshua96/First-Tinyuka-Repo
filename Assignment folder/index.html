Classic Pancake Recipe

fluffy, delicious pancakes perfect for brakfast and lanch.

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time : 10 mins
  • Cookin Time : 15 mins
  • Servings : 4 mins
  • Difficulty :Easy

Preview

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of all-purpose
  • 2 tablespoon of sugar
  • 1 tablespoon of baking powder
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoon of melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix dry Ingredients in a bowl
  2. Mix milk, egg, and melted butter in another bowl.
  3. Combine wet and dry Ingredients until smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and pour 1/4 of butter.
  5. Cook both side untill golden brown.

Watch Video

Tips

for extra fluffiness, let the butter rest for 5 minutes before cooking

Nutrition Facts(Bonus)
